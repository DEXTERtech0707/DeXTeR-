Cyber Security

Cyber Security is the protection of computers, networks, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, and damage. Its main goals are to ensure Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) of information. It protects against major threats like phishing, malware, ransomware, hacking, and data breaches using tools like firewalls, antivirus, encryption, strong passwords, and multi-factor authentication.
